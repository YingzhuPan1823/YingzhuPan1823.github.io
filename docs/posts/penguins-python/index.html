Introduction

In this post, I would like to figure out the difference of penguin’s body mass across different species.I will use python as the analysis tool to compare body mass across Adelie, Chinstrap, and Gentoo penguins.

Data source: Palmer Penguins, collected and made available by Dr. Kristen Gorman and Palmer Station Antarctica LTER. The data are available under the CC0 license. I access the data through the Python palmerpenguins package.

Original study: Gorman, K. B., Williams, T. D., and Fraser, W. R. (2014). Ecological sexual dimorphism and environmental variability within a community of Antarctic penguins (genus Pygoscelis). PLOS ONE, 9(3), e90081.

Clean the data

For this comparison, I need species and body mass. I remove rows with missing values in either column, while keeping rows that have missing values in unrelated columns.

mass_data = penguins.dropna(subset=["species", "body_mass_g"]).copy()

print("Original rows:", len(penguins))
print("Rows kept:", len(mass_data))
print("Rows removed:", len(penguins) - len(mass_data))

Summarize body mass by species

I calculate the sample size, mean, and median body mass for each species. Body mass is measured in grams.

mass_summary = (
    mass_data.groupby("species")
    .agg(
        sample_size=("body_mass_g", "count"),
        mean_mass_g=("body_mass_g", "mean"),
        median_mass_g=("body_mass_g", "median")
    )
    .round(1)
)

Compare body mass distributions

The boxplots show the median and spread of body mass within each species.

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

mass_data.boxplot(column="body_mass_g", by="species")
plt.suptitle("")
plt.title("Body mass by penguin species")
plt.xlabel("Species")
plt.ylabel("Body mass (g)")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

Conclusion

Among the 342 penguins with non-missing species and body mass, Gentoo penguins have the highest mean body mass (5,076.0 g). Adelie and Chinstrap penguins have similar mean body masses of 3,700.7 g and 3,733.1 g, respectively.

These results describe the penguins in this dataset. The boxplots also show variation in body mass within each species. This analysis also does not account for sex or other factors that may be associated with body mass.
